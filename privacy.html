Who We Are

ScoopAI ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile application that helps users track their daily nutrition, analyze meals through AI-powered photo recognition, and connect with personal trainers. ScoopAI is operated by ScoopAI Labs.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Category What we collect Why
Account Email address, display name, profile photo Authentication and personalization
Health & Diet Food logs, calorie/protein intake, supplement records, body goals Core nutrition tracking features
Photos Meal photos you choose to scan (processed, not permanently stored) AI food recognition
Device FCM push token, OS version, device type Push notifications
Usage App interaction logs, crash reports Improving app stability
Note: We do not collect payment information. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Health and diet data is treated as sensitive information.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the ScoopAI app and its features
  • Analyze meal photos using AI to identify food items and estimate nutrition
  • Generate weekly nutrition reports and progress insights
  • Enable trainer-member connections and communication features
  • Send push notifications you have opted into
  • Respond to your support requests and inquiries
  • Monitor for technical issues and ensure app security
